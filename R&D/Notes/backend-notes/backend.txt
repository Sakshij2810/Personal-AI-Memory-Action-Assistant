1. A recovery mechanism

We'll periodically check PostgreSQL for images that are still waiting for processing and ensure their jobs exist in BullMQ.
The flow will look like this:

       PostgreSQL
           |
           v
 Find images with
 status = uploaded
           |
           v
    For each image
           |
           v
 Check whether its
 BullMQ job exists
       /       \
     Yes        No
      |          |
    Skip       Add job
                 |
                 v
             Redis Queue
